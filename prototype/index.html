FAQ

Questions owners ask before the first call

GEO: clean Q&A gives AI engines answers to lift. Mark up with FAQPage schema.
What does a salon or spa business coach actually do?

A business coach works on the business side of your salon, spa or practice: pricing, marketing, booking, hiring and financial targets. You leave each session with specific changes to make that week, and we review the results together.

Do you only work with businesses in the Bay Area?

No. I'm based in Oakland, CA and meet East Bay and Bay Area clients in person when it helps, but most coaching happens over video with owners across the United States.

How fast will I see results?

Pricing and menu changes usually show up in revenue within the first 30 to 60 days. Lead generation and team systems take longer, typically one to two quarters, to compound.

Do you work with dental practices too?

Yes. Dental practices face the same problems as spas: an owner who does everything, uneven new-patient flow and unclear team roles. The systems carry over directly.

What happens on the free 15-minute call?

You tell me where the business is and where you want it to be. I'll tell you honestly whether coaching makes sense right now and which package fits. There is no pitch if it isn't a fit.
